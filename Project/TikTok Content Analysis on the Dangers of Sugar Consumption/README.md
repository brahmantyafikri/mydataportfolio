# Sugar Risk Content on TikTok

Fifty videos hand-coded: everyone on TikTok warns about sugar, almost nobody
offers an alternative.

**Communication study, Universitas Airlangga · 2025 · manual content coding, agenda-setting, cultivation theory, two-step flow**

> *Gula dan Risiko Kesehatan: Analisis Konten TikTok tentang Bahaya Konsumsi Gula*

## Context

TikTok is a primary health-information channel in Indonesia, but not all of it
is accurate. This study asks what the platform actually says about sugar — what
themes get airtime, what tone is used, what visual grammar carries the message,
and whether professionals, influencers and ordinary users frame the risk
differently.

## Method

Fifty TikTok videos on the health risks of sugar, coded by hand across five
axes: theme, tone, visual element, soundtrack and creator type. Results read
against three mass-communication theories — agenda-setting, cultivation theory,
and two-step flow of communication.

## Results

### Who is speaking

- 46 of 50 videos show a person on camera
- 72% male
- 80% aged 25–59; 15.8% aged 60+; 4.4% aged 18–24

### Theme — the gap

| Theme | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Physical health risk | 50% |
| Mental health impact | 40% |
| **Alternatives / solutions** | **10%** |

Half the content warns about physical harm, 40% about mental harm, and only one
video in ten offers a way out. **Plenty of warning, almost no solution** — an
open gap for any creator willing to fill it, especially health professionals.

### Tone

| Tone | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Educational | 66.67% |
| Sensational | 15.69% |
| Testimonial | 15.69% |
| Comedic | 1.96% |

Factual education dominates, which is better than the platform's reputation
would suggest — though a sixth of content still leans sensational.

### Visual grammar

| Element | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Text overlay | 44.86% |
| Animation | 26.17% |
| Compelling imagery | 24.30% |
| Charts / diagrams | 4.67% |

| Audio | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Instrumental only | 48% |
| None | 48% |
| Song | 4% |
| Trending sound | **0%** |

Not one video used a trending sound. These creators are prioritising the
message over the algorithm — which limits reach but suggests the content is
built to inform rather than to go viral.

### Same risk, three deliveries

| Creator type | How they frame it |
| --- | --- |
| Health professional (62%) | Medical evidence, clinical terminology, credibility |
| Influencer (24%) | Personal experience and testimony, relatability |
| Ordinary user | Fear and personal consequence |

Professionals supply the credibility; influencers supply the reach. Consistent
with two-step flow — information moves to the public through opinion leaders,
not directly.
